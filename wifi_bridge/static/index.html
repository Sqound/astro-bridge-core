      Automatic instrument switching

      Seestar

      Start a LAN search in the Seestar app. The bridge connects the only saved Seestar allowed for automatic switching.

      Vespera

      Open Singularity. Its connection request must reach the bridge; your home router may need a route. After switching, the app may need to search again.

      DWARF

      Opening the DWARFLAB home screen can request a switch. For the first connection, use Connect by IP Address with the bridge address shown in Networks. The app can remember it. A request to join the telescope’s own Wi-Fi may still come from DWARFLAB.

      Connection protection

      Requests prefer a free compatible connection, then a consenting quiet instrument. Reservations and owner locks always prevent replacement. No traffic does not prove that an exposure ended.

      DWARF discovery is passive only: it can request a connection but sends no discovery replies. Use the remembered Pi IP in the native app; first-time automatic discovery is unavailable.

      Vespera requests must reach the bridge through a route. Detection does not authenticate Singularity; the app may need to retry after Wi-Fi connection.

      ZWO mount · SynScan mount

      ZWO switching reacts to connection attempts on TCP 4030. SynScan switching reacts to version searches on UDP 11880. Discovery does not move the mount.

      How you connect

      Private Wi-Fi: stay on your usual network with Internet. Vespera may need a route on your router.

      Hotspot without Internet: join Astro Bridge for instrument access without configuring your router.

      Hotspot with Internet: join Astro Bridge; it shares Internet from your saved private Wi-Fi. No router route is needed for instrument access.

      Sharing needs separate available adapters for private Wi-Fi, hotspot and each instrument. Protected connections are preserved.

      Private-network access is optional. It allows outgoing connections from hotspot devices; it does not enable access in the reverse direction or relay device discovery.

      After changing modes, reconnect your phone to the hotspot to renew its network settings. If Internet is lost, instrument access remains available.

      Connection protection

      Connection protection

      A quiet exposure may still be active. Reserve a ready connection and renew before expiry, or use an owner lock that survives reboot. Logging out does not release reservations; restarting the bridge loses client reservations.

      Measurements describe matches on owned allowed forwarding paths, not guaranteed delivery or the end of an exposure.

      First installation, adopted unclassified sessions or lost counter history keep coverage unknown. After OS support is installed, only a later safe OS reboot can establish a fresh baseline; restarting the app is insufficient. Do not reboot during an exposure or while protections remain.

      Old images need OS support (conntrack 1.4.9 or later). An application update cannot install it. Keep an owner lock until coverage is established.

        Wi-Fi adapters

        Wi-Fi adapters

        A reserved adapter is used only for its assigned function, even when that function is stopped. Auto allows available adapters to be selected as needed. One adapter is needed for each simultaneous Wi-Fi connection. Two instruments, private Wi-Fi and a hotspot require four adapters. Missing reserved adapters are kept in the list until you change their role.

        Choose what each Wi-Fi adapter is used for. Roles are remembered after restart.

        Plug each Wi-Fi adapter directly into a USB port of the Raspberry Pi when it has enough ports, not into a hub. Behind a hub shared with a keyboard or another adapter, an adapter more often failed to get an address from a mount.

        Session data

        Session data

        Session data

        Astro Bridge does not include any ZWO key. 'Get the key' downloads only the part of the Seestar app package (from ZWO's own servers) that holds it — about 1.5 MB — and needs Internet on the bridge. The key is stored encrypted on this bridge and never leaves it; someone with the whole memory card could still recover it. With the key, Astro Bridge can read the Seestar's stacking progress (frames kept and dropped), exposures, GoTo results, board temperature and battery while your apps are closed. It never controls the Seestar.

        Supplying a key enables listening for profiles without a saved session-data choice. Your saved choices are preserved. You can turn listening off here. Event delivery while the maker app runs a plan and connection lifetime still need hardware testing.
